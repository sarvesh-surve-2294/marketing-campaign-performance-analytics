# 📊 Marketing Campaign Performance Analytics

An end-to-end Marketing Analytics project that analyzes campaign performance across multiple digital marketing platforms using **SQL**, **Python**, and **Power BI**. The project focuses on evaluating campaign effectiveness, platform performance, profitability, ROI, ROAS, and time-based trends to generate actionable business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Marketing teams invest significant budgets across multiple digital platforms to achieve objectives such as **Sales, Leads, Traffic, Reach, Engagement, and Video Views**. Understanding which campaigns and platforms deliver the highest return is essential for making informed marketing decisions.

This project analyzes marketing campaign data to answer key business questions, uncover performance trends, and provide data-driven recommendations through SQL analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

This project answers the following business questions:

- Which campaigns generate the highest revenue and profit?
- Which marketing platforms deliver the best ROI and ROAS?
- Which campaign objectives contribute the most revenue?
- How does campaign performance change over time?
- Which months and quarters perform the best?
- How should marketing budgets be allocated more efficiently?

# 📈 Key Business Insights

- Google Search generated the highest overall revenue and profitability.
- Sales campaigns delivered the strongest ROAS and ROI among all marketing objectives.
- Q4 consistently outperformed other quarters in revenue generation.
- Revenue showed steady growth from 2023 to 2024 before a slight decline in 2025.
- Sales and Lead Generation contributed nearly all business revenue.
- Several awareness-focused objectives consumed marketing spend without generating direct revenue, highlighting opportunities for budget optimization.
- Platform-wise performance comparison identified the most effective channels for each marketing objective.
